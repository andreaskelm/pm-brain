**Company:** Acme Analytics  
**Team:** Growth Platform — within B2B SaaS  
**Manager:** Jordan (Director of Product)  
**Key customer / partner:** Mid-market ops teams (500–2k employees) via Salesforce AppExchange

### What We Build

We help ops teams turn messy spreadsheet workflows into automated dashboards without hiring a data team.  
**Customer type:** B2B SaaS, self-serve trial + sales-assist for enterprise

### How I Work

**Cognitive Profile:**  
I think in systems and edge cases; I need time to process ambiguous trade-offs before committing. Visual summaries help me decide faster.

**Working Preferences:**
- Bullets and tables over long prose for options comparisons
- Written pre-reads before stakeholder meetings
- Explicit "what we decided / what we didn't decide" at the end of sessions
- Async feedback windows — don't expect instant replies on complex topics

**Strengths:**
- Connecting customer pain to business metrics
- Stakeholder mapping and pre-wiring decisions

**Challenges:**
- Context-switching between discovery and delivery in the same day
- Over-indexing on the loudest stakeholder vs. the riskiest assumption

**Communication Style:**
- Direct but not blunt — lead with the recommendation, then the reasoning
- Prefer Slack for quick alignment, docs for anything that might be revisited in 2+ weeks
