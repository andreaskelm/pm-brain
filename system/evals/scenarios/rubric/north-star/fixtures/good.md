# North Star Framework — Good specimen (rubric regression)

**Product:** B2B compliance workflow SaaS (mid-market) | **Product game:** PRODUCTIVITY

## North Star metric
**Weekly accounts completing a compliant review cycle** — unique accounts where an admin completes the weekly review checklist and actioned ≥1 flagged item within 48h.

**Why this metric:** Captures value delivered (teams staying compliant without rework), predicts retention and expansion better than signups or logins.

**Measurement:** Weekly aggregation in Product Analytics > Compliance Health. **Baseline:** 1,200 accounts/week. **Target:** 2,000 by end of Q4.

## Input metrics (teams can influence these)

| Input | How it drives North Star | Owner | Work connection |
|-------|--------------------------|-------|-----------------|
| First-session setup completion rate | Admins who finish setup in session 1 are 3× more likely to run weekly reviews | Onboarding squad | "Simplify setup checklist" initiative |
| Weekly digest open rate | Opens correlate with review completion (r=0.62) | Growth + Core | "Digest relevance" experiment |
| Median time to action flagged items | Faster action → higher completion rate | Workflow squad | "One-click action from digest" |

## Strategic alignment
Supports company priority: grow mid-market NRR by reducing churn from compliance friction.

## Operationalization
North Star reviewed in monthly product review; inputs mapped to Q3 OKRs.
