# PRD — Good specimen (rubric regression)

**Feature:** Weekly digest for mid-market ops teams  
**Audience:** VP Operations, 50–200 seat accounts

## Problem (outcome)
Mid-market ops teams batch compliance work weekly but lack a single view of what changed since last review. Outcome: reduce time-to-triage from 45 min to 15 min per weekly review.

## Success metrics
- Weekly review session duration (target: median ≤ 15 min by week 8)
- % of flagged items actioned within 48h (target: 60%)

## Assumptions
- Users already run weekly reviews (validated in 4 interviews)
- Email is the primary notification channel today (guess — not measured)

## Non-scope
- Real-time alerts
- Enterprise SSO customization

## Risks
- Digest noise increases ignore rate (second-order: teams revert to manual scans)
