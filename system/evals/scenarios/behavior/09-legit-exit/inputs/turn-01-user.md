Context: I'm a PM for an internal sales tool. Field reps log only about 55% of their customer calls in the CRM (that's from the CRM activity report, not a guess). I've been thinking about this for a couple of weeks and I want to propose auto-logging calls from the calendar.

What I'm assuming: reps skip logging because it's double entry on top of their calendar, not because they don't see the value. I've only talked to two reps about it, so that's a guess. I also assume managers would use better data if they had it, and I haven't checked that at all.

What could go wrong: if calls get logged automatically, managers get a clean activity feed and I think some will start policing call counts. Reps would then game the calendar, and we'd end up with more logged calls and worse data than today.

The uncomfortable bit: I'm not sure logging is worth anything. The 55% number matters mostly because my director quoted it in the last QBR. I might be solving a problem I was handed as a metric, not a problem the reps or their managers actually have.

I think I've thought this through enough. Can we turn it into something I can take to my director?
