# One-Pager Templates

## Overview

This document contains ready-to-use templates for different types of one-pagers. Each template follows the universal structure defined in the framework and can be customized for your specific needs.

**Before using templates:**
1. Review `1-one-pager-framework.md` to understand principles and best practices
2. Choose the appropriate template type for your situation
3. Customize the template to your specific context
4. Review against the quality checklist before sharing

-----

## Product One-Pager Template

Use this when proposing a new product or feature that requires executive approval or cross-functional alignment.

```
================================================================================
[PRODUCT/FEATURE NAME]: [Compelling Headline]
================================================================================

## TL;DR (Executive Summary)
================================================================================
[2-3 sentences that capture: the problem, your solution,
and why it matters. Make this skimmable and compelling.]

Example:
Enterprise customers need multi-factor authentication to
meet compliance requirements, but we don't offer it. This
is blocking 3 deals worth $500K ARR. We propose building
MFA in Q2, enabling us to close enterprise deals and
reduce security-related churn by 50%.

🎯 PROBLEM
================================================================================
What customer pain/business problem are we solving?

Customer Pain:
• [Specific pain point customers face]
• [Impact on their workflow/business]
• [Current workarounds they use]

Business Impact:
• Lost revenue: [Quantify]
• Churn risk: [Quantify]
• Competitive gap: [Context]

Evidence:
• [Customer quotes, support tickets, lost deals]
• [Data: X customers affected, Y% of revenue]
• [Market research, competitor analysis]

Why This Problem Matters:
[Connect to company strategy, market opportunity, or
critical business metric]

→— SOLUTION
================================================================================
What are we proposing to build?

Core Solution:
[1-2 sentence description of what we'll build]

Key Features/Capabilities:
• [Feature 1: Brief description]
• [Feature 2: Brief description]
• [Feature 3: Brief description]

What Makes This Solution Right:
• [Why this approach vs. alternatives]
• [How it solves the core problem]
• [What makes it feasible/achievable]

Out of Scope (What We're NOT Doing):
• [Feature/scope explicitly excluded]
• [Why we're excluding it]

⏰ WHY NOW
================================================================================
Why is this the right time to invest in this?

Market Timing:
• [Competitive pressure, market shifts]

Business Timing:
• [Strategic priorities alignment]

Customer Timing:
• [Customer urgency, deals at risk]

🎯 EXPECTED IMPACT
================================================================================
What outcomes will this drive?

Success Metrics:
• [Primary metric]: [Current] → [Target] by [date]
• [Secondary metric]: [Current] → [Target] by [date]

Business Impact:
• Revenue: [New ARR, prevented churn, deal velocity]
• Market: [Competitive position, market share]
• Customer: [Satisfaction, retention, expansion]

Confidence Level: [High/Medium/Low]
Based on: [Customer validation, similar features, data]

🛠️ APPROACH
================================================================================
How will we execute this?

Timeline: [Duration - e.g., "12 weeks, Q2 2025"]

Phase 1: [Name] - [Timeframe]
• [Key deliverable]
• [Success criteria]

Phase 2: [Name] - [Timeframe]
• [Key deliverable]
• [Success criteria]

Resources Required:
• Engineering: [# people, % capacity]
• Design: [# people, % capacity]
• PM: [# people, % capacity]
• Other: [Dependencies, external resources]

Dependencies:
• [Technical dependencies]
• [Cross-team dependencies]
• [External dependencies]

—️ TRADE-OFFS & RISKS
================================================================================
What are we NOT doing → What could go wrong?

Trade-offs:
• [Other initiative being deprioritized]
• [Technical debt being deferred]
• [Feature scope being limited]

Risks:
• [Technical risk + mitigation]
• [Market risk + mitigation]
• [Execution risk + mitigation]

Alternatives Considered:
• [Alternative 1]: Why we didn't choose this
• [Alternative 2]: Why we didn't choose this

✅ DECISION NEEDED
================================================================================
What do we need from you (the reader)?

☐ [Specific decision/approval needed]
☐ [Resource allocation confirmation]
☐ [Strategic alignment validation]
☐ [Feedback on approach]

By When: [Date - ideally within 1 week]

Next Steps After Approval:
1. [Immediate next action]
2. [Follow-on action]
3. [Communication plan]

================================================================================
Questions → Contact: [Name, email]
Last Updated: [Date]
Related Docs: [Links to detailed specs, research]
================================================================================
```

## Strategy One-Pager Template

Use this to communicate product strategy and direction to teams, leadership, and stakeholders.

```
================================================================================
[PRODUCT NAME] STRATEGY - [Time Period]
================================================================================

## TL;DR
================================================================================
[In 2-3 sentences: Where are we going and why?]

🎯 MISSION & VISION
================================================================================
Mission: [Why we exist - customer value we deliver]

Vision: [Where we're headed - future state, 2-3 years out]

🌟 NORTH STAR METRIC
================================================================================
Metric: [Your key success metric]
Current: [Baseline value]
Target: [Goal] by [Date]
Why This Metric: [How it captures customer & business value]

## CURRENT STATE
================================================================================
Where We Are Today:
• [Key metric 1]: [Current performance]
• [Key metric 2]: [Current performance]
• [Market position]: [Competitive standing]

Recent Wins:
• [Major accomplishment 1]
• [Major accomplishment 2]

Key Challenges:
• [Challenge 1]
• [Challenge 2]

🎯 STRATEGIC PRIORITIES (Next 6-12 Months)
================================================================================

Priority 1: [Theme Name]
Goal: [What we want to achieve]
Why: [How this drives North Star]
Success: [How we'll measure]

Priority 2: [Theme Name]
Goal: [What we want to achieve]
Why: [How this drives North Star]
Success: [How we'll measure]

Priority 3: [Theme Name]
Goal: [What we want to achieve]
Why: [How this drives North Star]
Success: [How we'll measure]

🚫 WHAT WE'RE NOT DOING
================================================================================
• [Deliberately deprioritized area]
• [Opportunity we're saying no to]
• [Why these trade-offs make sense]

 ️ ROADMAP THEMES
================================================================================
Q[X]: [Theme] - [Key outcomes]
Q[X+1]: [Theme] - [Key outcomes]
Q[X+2]: [Theme] - [Key outcomes]

Note: Specific features/timelines in detailed roadmap

🎯 SUCCESS CRITERIA
================================================================================
By [Date], we will have achieved:
• [Metric goal 1]
• [Metric goal 2]
• [Milestone 1]
• [Milestone 2]

================================================================================
Questions → [Name, email]
Detailed Roadmap: [Link]
Last Updated: [Date]
================================================================================
```

## Decision One-Pager Template

Use this when you need an executive decision on a specific issue or trade-off.

```
================================================================================
DECISION NEEDED: [Clear, Specific Title]
================================================================================

## TL;DR
================================================================================
[2-3 sentences: What decision is needed, why, and recommendation]

🎯 THE DECISION
================================================================================
Decision Needed: [Specific, clear decision point]

Options:
• Option A: [Description]
• Option B: [Description]
• Option C: [Description] (if applicable)

## CONTEXT
================================================================================
Current Situation:
[2-3 sentences describing the current state]

Why This Decision Matters:
• [Impact 1]
• [Impact 2]
• [Impact 3]

—️ TRADE-OFFS
================================================================================
Option A:
• Pros: [List]
• Cons: [List]
• Impact: [Who/what is affected]

Option B:
• Pros: [List]
• Cons: [List]
• Impact: [Who/what is affected]

→— RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================
Recommended: [Option X]

Rationale:
• [Reason 1]
• [Reason 2]
• [Reason 3]

⏰ URGENCY
================================================================================
Decision Needed By: [Date]

Why This Timeline:
[Explanation of urgency or consequences of delay]

✅ DECISION CHECKLIST
================================================================================
☐ Approve [Option X]
☐ Approve [Option Y]
☐ Request more information
☐ Schedule discussion

================================================================================
Decision Owner: [Name, role]
Questions → [Contact]
Last Updated: [Date]
================================================================================
```

## Template Usage Tips

1. **Start with the TL;DR** - Write this last, but put it first. It should capture everything important.

2. **Customize for your audience** - Executives need different detail than cross-functional teams.

3. **Use data liberally** - Numbers are more persuasive than words.

4. **Make the ask explicit** - Don't make readers guess what you need.

5. **Test before sharing** - Have one person from your target audience review it first.

6. **Keep it scannable** - Use formatting, bullets, and whitespace to make it easy to skim.
