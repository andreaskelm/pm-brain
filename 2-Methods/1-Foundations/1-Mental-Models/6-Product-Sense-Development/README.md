## The practice loop

Product sense grows from making calls explicit *before* you know the outcome, then looking honestly at what happened. The minimum:

1. **Decisions** — log real calls with a confidence level in [5-Growth/decisions.md](../../../../5-Growth/decisions.md). The agent offers this whenever you state one.
2. **Weekly** — one short note in [5-Growth/weekly/](../../../../5-Growth/weekly/README.md): what resolved, what you're carrying, what you're avoiding. The agent drafts it.
3. **Reps (optional)** — one 10-minute exercise from [2-practice-exercises.md](2-practice-exercises.md) when you want to train a specific muscle.

Monthly synthesis and quarterly self-assessment are in [3-Self-Reflection/](../../3-Self-Reflection/README.md); calibration scoring is in [8-calibration.md](../1-Decision-Making/8-calibration.md).
