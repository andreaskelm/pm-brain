## Product Sense Development

**Purpose:** Reference theory for developing product intuition through deliberate practice.

This folder holds the **theory and reference content** (framework, mental model bridge, AI product sense, meta-thinking). Coaching process files (prompts, evaluation, README entry) live in `system/coaching/`; practice templates live in `5-Growth/4-Coaching-Templates/`. It underpins how you use every other framework in `2-Methods/`.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Read the framework** — [`1-product-sense-framework.md`](1-product-sense-framework.md) for philosophy and method.
2. **Braindump before any framework** — use [`system/coaching/prompts.md`](../../../../system/coaching/prompts.md) and the golden rule in [`system/coaching/braindump.md`](../../../../system/coaching/braindump.md).
3. **Do one 10-minute rep** — pick an exercise from [`5-Growth/4-Coaching-Templates/1-daily-practice-exercises.md`](../../../../5-Growth/4-Coaching-Templates/1-daily-practice-exercises.md) and log it in `5-Growth/1-daily-log-YYYY-QX.md`.
4. **Then open an execution framework** — e.g. OKRs, PRDs, Opportunity Assessment under `2-Methods/`.

## How the practice system works

```text
Daily Practice (10 min)
 ?
Weekly Reflection (30 min)
 ?
Monthly Synthesis (60 min)
 ?
Growth Portfolio (evidence for reviews)
```

- **Daily (10 min)** — pick ONE exercise from `1-daily-practice-exercises.md` and log 1-2 insights in `5-Growth/1-daily-log-YYYY-QX.md`.
- **Weekly (30 min)** — copy `3-weekly-reflection-template.md` into `5-Growth/1-Learning-Log/YYYY-QX/week-##.md`; summarize decisions, patterns, next-week focus.
- **Monthly (60 min)** — use `4-monthly-synthesis-template.md` + `2-self-assessment-template.md`; move 1-2 highlights into `5-Growth/2-Growth-Portfolio/`.

## Theory → practice → execution

1. **Think first** — braindump with `system/coaching/prompts.md`.
2. **Structure second** — pick the right framework under `2-Methods/` (PRD, OKR, roadmap, discovery, etc.).
3. **Execute and reflect** — build under `3-Work/`, log and review in `5-Growth/`.

---

## When to use this

- You want to **deliberately improve product judgment**, not just ship a doc.
- You're about to make a **meaningful decision** (strategy, roadmap, PRD, big trade-off).
- You're stuck, circling a decision, or feel your thinking is shallow.
- You want **evidence of growth** for reviews or your own confidence.
