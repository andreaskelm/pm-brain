# Foundations Library

**What this file is:** Index of **everything in 1-Foundations only** — mental models (Decision-Making, Product-Thinking, Work-Levels, etc.) plus Bias and Self-Reflection. For frameworks **across** Strategy, Discovery, Execution, and Communication (by topic), see [1-frameworks-by-topic.md](../1-frameworks-by-topic.md) at the root of `2-Methods/`.

This library gives quick access to all mental models and foundational frameworks in the PM Brain system. Mental models are quick-reference thinking tools; frameworks here provide structured processes and templates.

## Full Frameworks

These are comprehensive frameworks with detailed processes, templates, and guidance.

### [Self-Reflection Framework](3-Self-Reflection/README.md)
Develop self-awareness, learn from experience, and improve product judgment through structured reflection prompts and exercises.

**Use when:**
- After completing frameworks or processes
- After major decisions or launches
- Weekly/monthly personal retrospectives
- When something didn't go as expected

### [Cognitive Bias Framework](2-Bias/README.md)
Recognize and mitigate cognitive biases that distort decision-making. Includes top 10 biases for PMs, detection methods, and debiasing strategies.

**Use when:**
- Before major decisions
- During prioritization
- When evaluating opportunities
- During strategy planning

## How This Wires Into the Coaching System

Two-tier architecture: some content is **always-on** (distilled into coaching lenses), the rest is **on-demand** (woken by conversation triggers). The wiring is declared in [MEMORY.md](../../system/MEMORY.md) and [ORCHESTRATION.md](../../system/ORCHESTRATION.md) — this section is the human-readable map of those decisions.

### Always-On (coaching lenses in AGENTS.md)

Encoded as coaching instincts active in every conversation, no explicit load step needed. See [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) — Coaching Lenses for the full list.

| Mental model | Always-on lens |
|---|---|
| Pre-mortems | "Pre-mortem instinct" — asks what would have to be true for this to fail |
| Assumptions Framework | "Assumptions vs facts" — prompts naming what is known vs guessed |
| Outcome vs Output | "Outcome vs output" — pulls back from deliverable-focus to impact |
| Bias Framework | "Bias interception" — fires upstream of all other lenses; biases corrupt inputs before reasoning starts |

### On-Demand (explicit wake triggers)

Load when the conversation touches the relevant area. All deep-linked to specific files — no folder guessing.

| Trigger | Load |
|---|---|
| Bias pattern needs unpacking beyond the lens | [2-Bias/1-bias-framework.md](2-Bias/1-bias-framework.md) |
| "Tactical vs strategic", "where should I focus", 60-30-10, roadmap horizon | [3-Work-Levels/1-tactical-operational-strategic.md](1-Mental-Models/3-Work-Levels/1-tactical-operational-strategic.md) + [3-sixty-thirty-ten.md](1-Mental-Models/3-Work-Levels/3-sixty-thirty-ten.md) |
| Decision reversibility, "one-way door", "can we undo this" | [1-Decision-Making/2-one-way-two-way-doors.md](1-Mental-Models/1-Decision-Making/2-one-way-two-way-doors.md) |
| Idea evaluation, discovery, "will users use it", "is it feasible", "is it viable" | [2-Product-Thinking/4-four-risks.md](1-Mental-Models/2-Product-Thinking/4-four-risks.md) |
| Team misalignment, "are we aligned", "everyone pulling different directions", before big initiative, "busy but nothing moving", high effort + low progress | [5-Team-Dynamics/1-alignment-check.md](1-Mental-Models/5-Team-Dynamics/1-alignment-check.md) |
| Product stage, "0 to 1", "scaling vs discovery", team structure decisions | [2-Product-Thinking/6-zero-to-one-vs-one-to-one-hundred.md](1-Mental-Models/2-Product-Thinking/6-zero-to-one-vs-one-to-one-hundred.md) |
