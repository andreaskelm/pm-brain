# 2-Qualitative-Research — Qualitative Research

Store qualitative research findings here — thematic analysis, usability study summaries, survey open-ended synthesis, and other non-interview qualitative outputs.

**Process:** Use discovery frameworks in [2-Methods/3-Discovery/](../../2-Methods/3-Discovery/README.md). **Storage:** Analysis documents live here; raw data (recordings, full datasets) stays external with links from your analysis docs.

See [4-Research/README.md](../README.md) for the full storage pattern.
