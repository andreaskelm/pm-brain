# Product-market fit (PMF)

PMF is **ongoing**, often **segment-specific**—not a one-time launch party. Synthesis tells you what job you're serving; PMF asks whether that job is served well enough that growth and retention compound.

## When to assess

- Post-MVP with enough users for retention curves or survey N≥40 qualified.
- Debate: scale marketing vs fix the product.
- Different segments may have different PMF—run survey and retention **by segment** when possible.

**Too early:** pre-MVP concept only—use interviews and validation instead.

## Core signals (qualitative)

**Weak PMF:** explaining the product constantly, long sales cycles, growth only when paid, lukewarm word of mouth.

**Strong PMF:** pull from market, usage grows with constrained supply, customers advocate, product used despite rough edges.

## Sean Ellis survey

**Question:** "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?"

- Very disappointed / Somewhat / Not disappointed

**Sample:** experienced core value, used 2+ times, recent use; aim 40–50+ responses.

**Rule of thumb:** ≥40% "very disappointed" among qualified users = PMF **signal** (not law)—interpret with retention.

**Cadence:** every 4–6 weeks pre-PMF; quarterly post-PMF.

## Metrics (triangulate)

| Area | What to look at |
|------|------------------|
| Retention | Cohort curves flattening; D30 meaningful for your product |
| Growth | Majority organic; growth survives pausing paid |
| Engagement | DAU/MAU, power users, frequency |
| Monetization | LTV/CAC, NRR where relevant |

Retention usually beats vanity press and funding as truth.

## Before vs after PMF

**Before PMF:** talk to users constantly, iterate fast, retention over acquisition, do things that don't scale, filter: "Does this help us find PMF?"

**After PMF:** scale distribution and ops carefully; don't assume permanence—keep interviewing; filter: "Does this help us scale without breaking fit?"

## Artifacts

- Quick gut check (minutes).
- Survey results doc with segment breakdown.
- Full quarterly assessment: survey + retention + growth + qual themes.

**Save as:** `4-Research/` or `3-Work/[initiative]/research/pmf-assessment-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`.

## Pitfalls

- Funding or press mistaken for PMF.
- Building for everyone—find who is **very disappointed** and why.
- Scaling acquisition while retention decays to zero.
- Ignoring segment where only one cohort has fit.
