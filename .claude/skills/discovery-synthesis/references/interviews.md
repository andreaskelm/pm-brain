# Research interviews

One-time or project-based conversations to learn what customers **did**, not what they say they might do. Heavily aligned with Continuous Discovery Habits and *The Mom Test* spirit: stories over opinions.

## When to use

- You need answers to specific questions before synthesis or validation.
- You're entering Discover (Double Diamond) or filling an OST with new evidence.
- You don't yet have a weekly interview habit—still plan outputs (snapshots).

**Not the main tool when** you already have 10+ unused snapshots (synthesize first), or you're only testing a built solution (lean toward [validation](validation.md)).

## Preflight (2 minutes)

- Primary research question (one sentence).
- Recruit screen: who had the problem **recently**?
- What you hope is **not** true (disconfirming hunt).
- Assumption list with evidence tier.

## Core principles

1. **Past behavior, not hypotheticals** — "Tell me about the last time…" not "Would you use…?"
2. **Stories, not pitches** — You're not selling; don't defend the roadmap.
3. **Jobs and context** — What were they trying to accomplish, with what constraints?
4. **Listen 80/20** — Silence is fine; probe with "what happened next?" and "give me a specific example."

## Question types

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|--------|
| Opening: role, typical day | Leading: "Don't you think X is better?" |
| STAR / five whys on real episodes | Yes/no: "Do you have this problem?" |
| JTBD: circumstance, push, pull, anxiety, habit | Feature wish lists (unless probing underlying need) |

## Structure (45–60 min typical)

- **Rapport (5–10 min):** broad, safe context.
- **Problem space (20–30 min):** specific episodes, workarounds, failures.
- **Outcomes (10–15 min):** what "good" looked like; emotions at key moments.
- **Close (5 min):** anything else; thank; optional follow-up.

## Biases to watch

**Yours:** confirmation, leading questions, anchoring on your product early.

**Theirs:** social desirability, recency, memorable extremes—ask for typical cases and multiple examples.

## Post-interview (within 24h)

1. Capture snapshot (see [continuous-discovery](continuous-discovery.md))—all stories, not a cherry-picked two.
2. Note surprises and contradictions (said vs did).
3. Tag for later synthesis (segment, use case, product area).
4. Store raw transcript/recording externally; link from snapshot in `4-Research/1-User-Interviews/` or `3-Work/[initiative]/research/`.

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Guide is open-ended and behavior-focused.
- [ ] You can state what would falsify your main hypothesis.
- [ ] Snapshot has quotes and step-by-step behavior.
- [ ] No solution pitch dominated the call.
