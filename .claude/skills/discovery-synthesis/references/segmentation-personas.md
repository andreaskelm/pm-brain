# Segmentation & personas

**Segments = strategy** (what to build, who to serve first, pricing/positioning). **Personas = execution** (marketing copy, UX empathy, stakeholder alignment). Segment by **job + context**, not demographics alone.

## When to segment

- You have ~15–30+ job stories from JTBD or rich snapshots.
- You need different features, messaging, or GTM for groups that share a job.
- Before creating HEART/demographic personas—define segments first.

**Don't segment yet** with zero job research (interview first). **Don't** create 10+ segments—consolidate to **3–5**, prioritize **1–2** to serve.

## Job clustering → segments

For each job story, tag:

- Desired outcome type, frequency, urgency, context (solo/team).
- Primary push/pull, current solution, WTP band.

Group jobs that could share ~80% of features, similar channels and WTP. Name 3–5 segments with a job statement each:

When [situation], I want to [progress], so I can [outcome].

Per segment document: forces, success criteria, who has the job (behavioral signals), competitive set (hired/fired), product & GTM implications, validation status.

**Save as:** `4-Research/segments/segment-[name]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md` or one doc per initiative.

## Validation

- **Qualitative:** 5–10 interviews per segment—do they recognize the job and competitive set?
- **Quantitative (optional):** survey with job-screening questions; check sizes.
- **Behavioral (optional):** usage aligns with segment proxy.

Outcomes: Validated / Revise / Merge / Split / Reject.

## Personas (execution)

After prioritizing segments (`prioritize` skill), create **1–2 personas per priority segment**.

**HEART (human-centered):** Human, Emotions, Actions, Results, Triggers—concise bullets.

**JOBS persona variant:** Job, trigger, outcome, obstacles, context—action-first.

Personas are **not** a substitute for segments: a persona without a job is cardboard.

**Save as:** initiative or `1-Context/` stakeholder avatars if maintained—follow repo conventions.

## Red flags

- Segments are job titles only ("SMB owners").
- Same messaging fits all segments—merge them.
- Can't identify segment membership (survey, behavior, or context).
- PMF or roadmap debates without naming which segment you're serving.

## Example (compressed)

Three job families from interview synthesis collapse to one segment when they share daily capture urgency, mobile context, and willingness to switch from phone notes—different demographics, same job. A fourth family (team alignment on decisions) stays separate: different frequency, buyers, and feature set (~20% overlap, not 80%).

## Handoffs

JTBD → segmentation → prioritize segment → personas → PMF by segment ([pmf](pmf.md)). Problem priorities may differ per segment—tie to OST branches in [problem-solution](problem-solution.md).
