# Opportunity Assessment — quick check (creation + light review)

**Full scored evaluation:** `2-Methods/3-Discovery/4-Opportunity-Assessment/3-opportunity-assessment-evaluation.md` with process in `system/EVALUATION.md`. Use this file only for in-turn red/green scanning and fast checks — not as a substitute for the full rubric.

## Red flags

- **No clear hypothesis** — opportunity statement vague or missing (often buried in Objective)
- **Assumptions not identified** — no High/Med/Low confidence on assumptions
- **Missing evidence plan** — no research plan in **What We Should Research**
- **Vague opportunity** — too broad to test
- **Solution bias** — solution ideas before problem/objective/metrics are clear
- **Missing decision criteria** — no kill/pivot/commit thresholds
- **No customer context** — missing target customer or JTBD
- **Missing success metrics** — no baselines/targets/guardrails

## Green flags

- Clear objective and why now
- Assumptions documented with confidence levels (High/Med/Low)
- Evidence plan with methods and decision criteria
- Target customer with JTBD context
- Success metrics with baselines and targets
- Kill/pivot/commit thresholds defined before evidence
- Risks across desirability, viability, feasibility, usability (plus compliance/dependencies when relevant)
- At least two high-level solution concepts; out of scope stated
