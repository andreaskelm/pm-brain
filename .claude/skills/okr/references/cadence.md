# OKR Cadence and Confidence

OKRs are only useful if they change what the team does mid-cycle. The rhythm below is the minimum that makes that happen. If the org won't hold all of it, keep the weekly check-in and drop the rest.

## Confidence scale (weekly)

| Score | Meaning | What it triggers |
|---|---|---|
| **2** | On track | Keep going. Still cite the evidence. |
| **1** | At risk | Plan an adjustment this week: what would get it back to 2? |
| **0** | Off track | Investigate now: wrong tactic, wrong target, or wrong KR? |

Every score needs evidence: a number, a test result, user feedback. "Feels fine" is not evidence. Confidence is a forecast, so it should move before the progress number does.

**Worked example**

```text
Week 1–4: O1-KR1 confidence 2. Adoption tracking to plan.
Week 5:   confidence 1. Adoption 30% below target; feedback says onboarding is confusing.
          Next: test a simplified onboarding flow with 20 new accounts next week.
Week 6:   simplified flow lifts completion; confidence back to 2.
```

Course-corrected in week 6 instead of discovering the miss in week 13.

## The rhythm

- **Weekly (20–30 min):** KR status, confidence with evidence, risks and blockers, the next action or assumption test. Same time every week, or it dies.
- **Mid-cycle (1–2 h):** per objective, decide **double down, pivot, or stop**. Is the objective still the right one given what you've learned? Log any material change (new target, dropped KR) in the decision log, with the reason. Changing a KR mid-cycle is fine; changing it silently isn't.
- **End of cycle (2–3 h):** grade each KR 0.0–1.0; objective score = average of its KRs. Write what worked, what didn't, and what you'd measure differently. Archive the set.
- **Next-cycle planning:** start from last cycle's learnings and grades, not a blank page.

## Grading

Grade against the target, with the threshold as the line between "moved it" and "didn't". Moving 5,000 → 7,400 against a target of 8,000 is 0.8, and above the 6,500 threshold.

How to read the totals:
- **Everything at 1.0:** targets were too soft. Next cycle, stretch.
- **Around 0.6–0.7 on stretch KRs:** healthy, *if* missing is safe in this org.
- **Everything below 0.3:** either the targets were fantasy or the strategy is wrong. Worth finding out which.
- **Committed KRs** (the ones the org expects to land) should come in near 1.0. Grade them separately from stretch ones.

## Calibrating

At the end of each cycle, compare the confidence trend with the final grades. If KRs sat at 2 until week 11 and then landed at 0.3, the team's confidence is optimism, and you now have evidence instead of a feeling. Raise it in the retro, not in a review.
