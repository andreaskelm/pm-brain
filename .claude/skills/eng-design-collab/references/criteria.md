# Engineering and Design Collaboration Criteria

Flags-only checklist for discovery, negotiation, and handoff. Name the flag, ask one question, suggest the fix.

## Red flags

- **No shared problem** — solution spec before eng/design understand the job
- **Fixed iron triangle** — date, scope, and quality all claimed immovable
- **Feasibility after PRD** — full doc written before a spike or eng conversation
- **Estimate shopping** — re-asking until someone gives the number leadership wants
- **Design as final polish** — UX brought in after architecture and API shape locked
- **Missing non-goals** — scope grows by omission
- **Debt unnamed** — "we'll clean it up later" with no trigger or sponsor
- **Handoff as read-aloud** — no time for risks, no explicit asks, no owners
- **Silent misalignment** — eng and design heard different priorities; no written recap
- **Ops ignored** — no rollout, monitoring, support, or on-call plan
- **PM as ticket chaser** — collaboration only about status, not tradeoffs
- **Stakeholder promises smuggled in** — commitments not labelled in scope or debt

## Green flags

- Problem-first discovery with time-boxed spikes and design on risky flows
- Explicit tradeoff: what's flexible (date, scope, quality) and what isn't
- Non-goals and guardrails in the same doc eng/design sign off on
- Tech debt framed as a loan with benefit, cost, repay trigger, sponsor
- Handoff packet with open questions tagged by discipline
- Definition of done includes instrumentation, rollback, and design QA
- Written recap within 48 hours with changed assumptions visible
- Negotiation uses criteria (learning, risk, reversibility) not rank
