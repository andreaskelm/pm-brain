# Capture criteria

Flags only. A filed bit is judged by whether the user thought before the write, not by how complete the page looks.

## Red flags

- **Dump and file** — a paste was classified, fanned out, and written before they named the type, know vs guess, and landing spot
- **Task-shaped bullet** — "email X", "update the ticket", "what I did today"
- **Missing `landed:`** — the bit is an open loop dressed up as a note
- **Missing provenance** — a load-bearing claim with no `documented` / `verbal` / `hunch` / `industry` tag
- **Hypothesis without the floor** — a new bet file exists and the deciding assumption, know vs guess, a risk, and an uncomfortable thought were never said
- **Auto-logged decision** — a `5-Growth/decisions.md` row written without a confidence percent and an explicit yes
- **Invented home** — a new hypothesis opened just to park an observation
- **Invented person** — an avatar created from one bit
- **Second schema** — a `_SCHEMA.md` or a copied template inside `3-Work/`

## Green flags

- They named the type in their own words
- Know and guess are separate, and the guess is not smuggled in as a fact
- `landed:` points at a real file, or says `dropped` on purpose
- A new hypothesis carries a cheapest test and a decision trigger
- A scored decision has a confidence percent they agreed to
- Refusals are visible: tasks, mushy bits, and silent fan-out did not get files
