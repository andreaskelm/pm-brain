# 5-Growth

Your track record. Three places, and that's the practice.

| File | What | Who writes it |
|------|------|---------------|
| [decisions.md](decisions.md) | Every call worth remembering: confidence, reopen trigger, outcome, calibration score | The agent offers a row whenever you state a decision with a confidence level; you confirm |
| [weekly/](weekly/README.md) | One short note per week: decisions, live assumptions, drift, what you're avoiding | The agent drafts it in the weekly review; you correct it |
| [showcase.md](showcase.md) | One page for 1:1s and performance talks: a few decisions, one pattern, one next focus | The agent drafts from the log and recent weeklies; you edit before you share |

Earlier versions had daily logs, a growth portfolio, a research log, and a dashboard. Nobody kept them up. Product sense still grows the same way: make the call explicit *before* you know the outcome, look at the misses, and keep a short page you can actually bring to a manager.

**The loop:** log the bet in [decisions.md](decisions.md) → close the week from [weekly/template.md](weekly/template.md) → when something is worth telling, one line on [showcase.md](showcase.md). Background on scoring: [calibration](../2-Methods/1-Foundations/1-Mental-Models/1-Decision-Making/8-calibration.md). Longer monthly or quarterly pass: [self-reflection](../2-Methods/1-Foundations/3-Self-Reflection/README.md).

**Research insights** go with the evidence, in [4-Research/](../4-Research/README.md) or `3-Work/[initiative]/research/`, not here.

**Privacy:** [decisions.md](decisions.md) in the repo is the scaffold (one example row). Weekly notes (`YYYY-Www.md`) and a filled [showcase.md](showcase.md) are personal. In private or team mode those paths are gitignored; the scaffold is tracked so a new clone still has a log to write in. [weekly/README.md](weekly/README.md) and [weekly/template.md](weekly/template.md) stay in the repo. See [docs/setup.md](../docs/setup.md).
