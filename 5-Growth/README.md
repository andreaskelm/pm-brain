# 5-Growth

Your track record. Two things live here:

| File | What | Who writes it |
|------|------|---------------|
| [decisions.md](decisions.md) | Every call worth remembering: confidence, reopen trigger, outcome, calibration score | The agent offers a row whenever you state a decision with a confidence level; you confirm |
| [weekly/](weekly/README.md) | One short note per week: decisions, live assumptions, drift, what you're avoiding | The agent drafts it in the weekly review; you correct it |

That's deliberately all. Earlier versions had daily logs, learning logs, a growth portfolio, a research log and a dashboard. Nobody kept them up, and the empty templates made the system feel like homework. Product sense grows from making calls explicit *before* you know the outcome and looking honestly at the misses. These two files are the minimum that does that.

**Why it matters:** "product sense" usually means intuition plus hindsight storytelling. A decisions log with confidence levels turns it into a track record — when you say 70%, are you right 7 times out of 10? Background: [calibration](../2-Methods/1-Foundations/1-Mental-Models/1-Decision-Making/8-calibration.md).

**Research insights** go with the evidence, in [4-Research/](../4-Research/README.md) or `3-Work/[initiative]/research/`, not here. **Deeper reflection** (monthly synthesis, self-assessment, post-project reflection): [2-Methods/1-Foundations/3-Self-Reflection/](../2-Methods/1-Foundations/3-Self-Reflection/README.md).

**Privacy:** both files are personal. In private or team mode they're gitignored. See [docs/setup.md](../docs/setup.md).
