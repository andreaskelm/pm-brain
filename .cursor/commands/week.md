# Week planning

Plan the week: Monday (last-week retrospective, this-week priorities, team sync, calendar gardening), Wednesday (progress check, course correct, team pulse), Friday (retrospective, **drift sweep**, stakeholder update, prep next week).

**Reference:** @2-Methods/4-Execution/1-Daily-Execution-And-Rituals/2-weekly-cadence.md — use it for the full routine, templates, and output format.

**Output:** Week theme, Monday plan (primary goal, deliverables, focus blocks, watch items), Wednesday check-in (progress, course corrections), Friday wrap (shipped, learnings, carried over, stakeholder update, drift sweep findings).

## Friday drift sweep (brain-wide, ~10 min)

Run this as part of Friday close — before or after the week retrospective. Scan episodic logs for drift; route dated findings to the learning log.

**Load and scan:**
- `5-Growth/3-Product-Judgment-Test/forecast-log.md` — unresolved bets; reopen triggers that may have fired based on this week's signals
- `5-Growth/2-prioritization-decision-log.md` — recent decisions; reopen triggers vs. new evidence
- `5-Growth/3-research-insight-log.md` — insights that contradict prior decisions or open assumptions
- `5-Growth/1-Learning-Log/` — current week's reflection; **live assumptions to revisit** block
- Relevant `3-Work/[initiative]/` decision files and `1-Context/` if strategy or stakeholder context shifted this week

**Flag:**
1. **Fired reopen triggers** — stored "what would change my mind" conditions that new evidence may satisfy
2. **Stale beliefs** — assumptions in the live-assumptions block unchallenged for 3+ weeks, or forecasts/decisions with no revisit in 6+ weeks
3. **Contradictions** — new entries this week that cut against prior logged decisions or forecasts
4. **Weak evidence** — load-bearing claims resting on verbal/hunch/industry only where documented evidence was expected

**Output:** 3–5 bullets max — what's solid, what needs revisiting, one recommended action. Offer to update the learning log or flag a decision for meta_reflection.

On Friday, when you do your weekly reflection, optionally add the **"PM Brain This Week"** block from the weekly reflection template (used / frustrated / missing / one small action) so you keep lightweight feedback on how the repo and agent are working for you.
