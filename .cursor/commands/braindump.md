I need to think through a product decision. Use the **product_sense** workflow from [system/coaching/README.md](../../system/coaching/README.md):

1. **Stay in braindump mode**
   - Do **not** suggest any frameworks or templates yet.
   - Ask me 3–5 open-ended, situation-specific questions drawn from [system/coaching/prompts.md](../../system/coaching/prompts.md).
2. **Check context**
   - Ask whether I want to bring in any relevant context from `1-Context/`, `4-Research/`, or `3-Work/`.
3. **Probe assumptions**
   - Help me separate what I know from what I’m guessing.
   - Surface at least one risk/second-order effect and one uncomfortable thought (per `system/coaching/braindump.md`).
4. **Only after braindump is sufficient**
   - Summarize what you’ve heard.
   - Propose 1–2 candidate frameworks that might fit, but **only after** the braindump-sufficient checklist is met.
